Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 (waiting list added; first version November 3, 2026)

This policy explains how Korea Real Talk ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website askhanalive.com, our live shows and our social media accounts (together, the "Services").

1. Information we collect

  • Public live chat messages. When you post in the public chat of our live streams (for example on YouTube), we receive your display name and message from the platform so that Hana can read and answer questions on air.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Waiting list. If you join the TalkWeGo waiting list by email, we keep your email address (and the country or Korean level you choose to tell us) only to tell you when the app opens and to send your invitation. Reply "remove" at any time and we will delete it.
  • Account connections for publishing. We connect our own accounts on platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and X to publish our videos and posts. We do not use these connections to collect data about viewers.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may log standard server data (such as IP address and browser type) for security. This website does not use advertising cookies or trackers.

2. How we use information

  • To run live shows: chat messages are shown on screen and may be answered by Hana.
  • To reply to your messages and support requests.
  • To publish and manage content on our own social media accounts.
  • To keep the Services safe and to comply with law.

3. AI processing

Hana is an AI character. To generate her replies, the text of selected public chat messages is sent to AI service providers that process it on our behalf (language model, voice and video generation). We do not send email addresses or other contact details to these providers, and we do not use your messages to build profiles about you.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Services (hosting, AI processing, streaming platforms), when required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

Live chat messages appear in recordings of our streams for as long as the recording is published. Our internal logs of chat messages are kept for up to 90 days. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Your choices and rights

You can delete your chat messages on the platform where you posted them. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing hello@askhanalive.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We are based in the Republic of Korea. Our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, with appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date.
